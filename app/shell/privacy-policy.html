Privacy Policy

Sapiens4

Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. Overview

Sapiens4 is designed to give users control over their AI-powered workflows and application data.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed when you use Sapiens4, including local storage, third-party integrations, and usage telemetry.

2. Application Data

Sapiens4 stores workspace information, conversations, tasks, settings, and related application data locally on your device.

Certain information may be transmitted to external services when required by features you use, such as AI providers or remote access.

3. AI Processing

When using AI features, relevant information may be shared with the configured AI provider to process requests and perform tasks.

Data processed by third-party providers is subject to their respective privacy policies and practices.

4. Usage Telemetry

Sapiens4 collects limited technical and usage events to understand product usage, improve functionality, and maintain reliability.

Telemetry may include application events, timestamps, software information, and technical identifiers.

Product telemetry is not intended to collect conversation content, prompts, or the contents of user files.

Usage telemetry can be disabled at any time in the application Settings. Disabling telemetry stops future telemetry collection but does not automatically remove previously collected records.

5. Data Usage

Information processed by Sapiens4 is used to support application functionality, improve the product, maintain reliability, and diagnose technical issues.

Data collected through telemetry is handled separately from the content of your AI conversations and workspaces.

6. Data Storage and Retention

Application data is primarily stored on your device.

Information transmitted to external services may be processed or retained according to the purpose of the service and its applicable policies.

Sapiens4 telemetry is stored on infrastructure operated for the project and retained according to our published retention policy.

7. User Controls

You can manage application permissions, configure integrations, and disable usage telemetry through the available application settings.

Some features require external processing to operate and may not function without it.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy as Sapiens4 evolves or as legal requirements change.

The latest version will be available through our official website.
